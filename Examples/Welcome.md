# Markdown, at home on your Mac.

A quiet reader for your everyday documents. **Fast to open. Easy to read.**

## Made for reading

SSMV uses native macOS text, selection, search, and window controls. Follow your system appearance, or choose **Light** or **Dark** in the View menu.

- Open a Markdown document with **⌘O**.
- Search the page with **⌘F**.
- Adjust text size with **⌘+** and **⌘−**.
- Reload a changed file with **⌘R**.

## The small details

> A good tool gets out of your way.

Read **bold text**, *emphasis*, ~~strikethrough~~, and `inline code` without leaving the page.

```swift
let reader = "SSMV"
print("Hello, \(reader)")
```

### Your next steps

1. Put SSMV in Applications.
2. Select a Markdown file in Finder and choose Get Info.
3. Choose SSMV under Open with, then Change All.
